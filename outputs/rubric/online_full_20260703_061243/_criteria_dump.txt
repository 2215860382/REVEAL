GENERAL (3):
  - evidence_coverage: Evidence covers the key event, time window, state change, comparison target, or causal link needed to verify or exclude each option.
  - evidence_specificity: Evidence explicitly grounds the relevant person, object, action, scene, speaker, on-screen text, or spatial relation rather than a generic scene.
  - evidence_consistency: Evidence is internally consistent and contains enough contrary evidence to exclude options that conflict with the video.

### Action Reasoning (104):
  - inference_grounding: Evidence explicitly states or denies the logical link (inference, speculation, deduction) claimed in the option, rather than merely presenting the premises or the conclusion separately.
  - pathway_continuity: Evidence explicitly describes the complete sequence of steps, intermediate locations, or mechanisms required to traverse the specific path, route, or process claimed in the option, rather than merely identifying the start and end points.
  - spatial_scope_verification: Evidence explicitly demonstrates the full spatial extent, territorial boundaries, or geographic distribution claimed in the option, rather than merely identifying isolated locations or a single point within the claimed area.
  - modal_claim_grounding: Evidence explicitly validates the modality (possibility, probability, speculation) asserted in the option, rather than merely presenting the constituent elements or a different instance that implies it implicitly.
  - contrast_with_standard: Evidence explicitly establishes the deviation or difference between the observed state and the expected, traditional, or standard state required to verify or exclude options claiming a specific alteration, anomaly, or non-standard attribute.
  - thematic_synthesis: Evidence explicitly states the central theme, main idea, or overarching summary and clearly distinguishes it from specific supporting details, examples, or sub-topics, ensuring the main idea is not obscured or confused with a specific instance.
  - misconception_attribution: Evidence explicitly attributes a specific false belief, misunderstanding, or gap in knowledge to the target agent, rather than merely stating the correct fact or describing the agent's actions without reference to their mental state.

### Spatial Perception (3):
  - joint_visual_co_presence: Evidence shows the target entities simultaneously within the same visual frame or continuous shot, enabling direct observation of their relative spatial relationship (e.g., facing direction, distance, alignment) rather than inferring it from separate, disjointed appearances.
  - facing_direction_resolution: Evidence explicitly resolves the facing direction or body orientation of the target entities relative to each other or the camera, rather than merely confirming their presence or describing unrelated actions.
  - explicit_spatial_intent: Evidence explicitly states the intended spatial placement, location, or arrangement of the target object relative to other entities, rather than relying solely on visual inference from the current state or generic scene context.
